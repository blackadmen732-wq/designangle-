Bottom sheet, drag to dismiss

The sheet rises from the bottom edge and stops at half or full height. Drag or flick it down to close; Done stays at the top, in reach, and Escape closes it too. The browser's own scrolling moves it, so it follows the finger with native momentum. Built on pure-web-bottom-sheet.
