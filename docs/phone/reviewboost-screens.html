ReviewBoost, on a phone.

Four screens in ReviewBoost's own system: warm white, Geist over Instrument Serif, black for the single action, green only for "good". Built to the rule the design review already set — where you are, what is happening, then one black button for what is next.

Why these screens and not another Netflix. The pattern work so far came from two dark media apps where one action dominates. This is light, dense and a utility: several numbers matter at once, and the owner is scanning, not browsing. It is the case where "make one thing huge and cut the rest" stops being the right instruction — so it is the honest test of the method.
